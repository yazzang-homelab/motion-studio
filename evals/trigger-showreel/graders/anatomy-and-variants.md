The user quoted the viral prompt "make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion designer you are, like it's your showreel for a résumé. go all out." and asked why it works and for three variations that come out genuinely different.

PASS if the reply does both:
1. Explains at least three of these four reasons: the short fixed duration fits a handful of shots in one pass; the model itself is the subject, so it shows techniques and has no product content to get wrong; "showreel for a résumé" names a genre with known conventions (fast cuts, a new technique per shot, best work first); "go all out" pushes effort or ambition.
2. Gives at least three variant prompts that each change something structural, such as a persona (for example a niche studio), a genre or story instead of a technique list, a hard constraint (one accent color, no corner text, a format or duration), or a different technique or medium. Three rewordings of the same request, or variants that differ only by adjectives, do not count.

FAIL if the explanation is missing or vague, or if fewer than three structurally different variants are given.
