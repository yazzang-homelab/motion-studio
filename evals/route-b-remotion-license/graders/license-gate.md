The user runs a 12-person for-profit startup and wants to switch its launch videos to Remotion.

PASS if the reply does all three:
1. States that Remotion is not free for this team: its free license covers individuals and for-profit companies with up to 3 employees (or small organizations), so a 12-person company needs a paid Company License.
2. Puts that license check before the switch (sort it out first, or confirm eligibility before installing or building).
3. Describes a concrete handoff to Remotion, such as scaffolding a Remotion project, using Remotion's agent skills or plugin, rendering a named composition with npx remotion render, or keeping the same shot list, springs and beat grid.

FAIL if the license issue is missing, if it says Remotion is free for a company of this size, or if it gets the employee threshold wrong.
